## Brand & Style

This design system channels the sensory intensity and culinary precision of an authentic artisan Korean charcoal barbecue house. The design philosophy merges **Tactile Industrial Craft** with **Modern Bold Restraint**—evoking the glow of burning briquettes (*yeontan*), the searing hiss of cast-iron grills, and the warm patina of handmade earthenware.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Grounded & Artisanal:** Feels rooted in heritage, firecraft, and tactile authenticity rather than sterile corporate dining.
- **Bold & High-Contrast:** Punchy, confident typography anchored by deep soot blacks, balanced by glowing embers and natural parchment tones.
- **Raw Industrial Warmth:** Clean, structured grid architecture softened by textural parchment cards, muted zinc lines, and focused warmth.

### Audience
Discerning food enthusiasts, culinary travelers, and modern diners who value traditional firecraft, premium meats, and uncompromising atmosphere.

## Colors

The palette is derived from the elemental fire pit: glowing yeontan coals, natural butcher parchment, and blackened cast iron.

- **Primary (`#DC3823` - Ember Vermilion):** The focal heat source. Reserved for active interactive accents, key action triggers, branding moments, and spice indicators.
- **Secondary (`#D97724` - Roasted Amber):** The warmth of slow roasting. Applied to secondary interactive states, badge tags, subtle badges, and flame glows.
- **Tertiary (`#EAE4D9` - Parchment Cream):** The warmth of unbleached kraft and handmade hanji paper. Used for elevated card surfaces, contrast tags, and stark editorial breaks within dark layouts.
- **Neutral (`#111111` - Charcoal Black):** The primary canvas ground, representing soot, blackened timber, and heavy cast iron.
- **Named Colors:**
  - `grill-steel` (`#262626`): Structural container borders and grill dividers.
  - `soot-dark` (`#181818`): Layered surface containers slightly elevated above canvas black.
  - `ash-mist` (`#8C857B`): Secondary typographical tone for metadata, descriptions, and provenance.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the bold, industrial geometric angles of **Space Grotesk** with the fluid, rounded comfort of **Plus Jakarta Sans** (evoking the circular, approachable shapes reminiscent of Korean Cafe24 Ssurround typefoundry styles).

- **Space Grotesk (Headlines & Labels):** Provides an assertive, stencil-like industrial structure. All uppercase styling on small labels reinforces the artisanal smokehouse, butcher-cut stamp aesthetic.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Reading text):** Delivers organic warmth and high legibility across dark background modes without inducing eye strain.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 12-column responsive grid on desktop and tablet, shifting to a 4-column structure on mobile devices.

- **Rhythm & Structure:** Inspired by architectural grill slats and exposed restaurant rafters, content items align with clear vertical and horizontal dividing rules.
- **Canvas Boundaries:** Outer margins preserve generous room for heavy atmospheric contrast. 
  - Desktop: `3rem` (48px) margins with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.
  - Tablet: `2rem` (32px) margins with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.
  - Mobile: `1rem` (16px) margins with `1rem` (16px) gutters.
- **Asymmetric Tension:** Feature hero sections pair heavy full-width charcoal zones against compact, high-density parchment product sheets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on generic translucent dropshadows. Instead, elevation is communicated through **structural tonal layering** and **heat-tinted ambient reflections**:

1. **Base Surface:** Level 0 sits at Charcoal Black (`#111111`).
2. **Container Tier (Soot-Dark):** Raised cards and layout segments rest at `#181818`, edged with a crisp 1px stroke of Grill Steel (`#262626`).
3. **Parchment Cutout Tier:** Special menu highlights, butcher specials, and receipt modules sit on a flat Parchment Cream (`#EAE4D9`) plane with dark charcoal typography (`#111111`), creating tactile physical contrast.
4. **Ember Glow (Ambient Depth):** Focused interactive hover states and glowing primary indicators feature a delicate, diffused colored shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(220, 56, 35, 0.35)`.
5. **Dividers & Seams:** Hairline 1px borders mimic industrial angle iron and slatted iron griddles.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** edge profile. 

- Standard interactive components (buttons, input fields, badges) use a minimal `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Cards, menu containers, and modals employ `0.5rem` (8px).
- This crisp, low-radius curvature references cut timber, steel-stamped plates, and solid cast briquettes. Full pill-shaped radiuses are strictly avoided except for counter indicator dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Ember):** Filled with `#DC3823`, white uppercase `label-md` typography in Space Grotesk, 0.25rem corner radius. Hover triggers slight ember bloom (`0 6px 20px rgba(220, 56, 35, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Iron Outline):** Transparent background, 1px solid border in `#8C857B`, parchment cream text. Transitions to `#262626` background on hover.
- **Artisan Kraft Variant:** Parchment Cream (`#EAE4D9`) background with Charcoal (`#111111`) heavy text, used for physical tasting set reservations.

### Cards & Menu Modules
- **Charcoal Surface Card:** Background `#181818`, 1px solid `#262626` outline, padding `space-lg`.
- **Specialty Meat Tag (Parchment Card):** Solid `#EAE4D9` background, inset text in Charcoal Black `#111111`, stamped with an Ember Vermilion border tag indicating beef cuts, marbling score, or charcoal roast specs.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags (e.g., "Prime Cut", "Yeontan Grilled"):** Uppercase `label-sm`, letter-spacing `0.12em`, enclosed in a 1px border of `#DC3823` with a 10% alpha fill of Ember Vermilion.

### Form Inputs
- **Text Fields:** Background `#181818`, border 1px solid `#262626`, text in `#EAE4D9`. Active focus state sharpens the border to `#DC3823` without outer glow rings. Placeholder text in `#8C857B`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 16px geometry with 2px radius. Inactive states feature `#262626` borders. Selected states fill with `#DC3823` featuring an off-white checkmark.

### Additional Artisan Components
- **Marbling / Doneness Gauge:** A stepped multi-segment segmented bar in `#262626` filled sequentially with `#DC3823` to represent grill heat and cut tiers.
- **Grill Stamp Divider:** A 1px horizontal line in `#262626` broken by an Ember-colored geometric flame icon or woodcut monogram.
